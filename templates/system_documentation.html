Travel Expense Guidelines

This guide explains how to properly submit travel expenses in the EPV system, including validation rules, required fields, and best practices.

Travel Types

Local Conveyance

Travel within the same city

  • School/office visits
  • Local meetings
  • City transportation
Domestic Travel

Travel between cities in India

  • Mumbai to Pune
  • Mumbai to Nagpur
  • Inter-city meetings
  • Training sessions

Travel Validation Rules

Date Validation
  • Travel Date: Must be within the expense period (From Date to To Date)
  • Future Dates: Cannot select dates in the future
  • Past Limit: Cannot be more than 30 days in the past
Location Validation
  • From Location: Required for all travel types
  • To Location: Required for all travel types
  • Distance (KM): Required for Local and Domestic travel

Travel Mode Guidelines

Travel Mode When to Use Documentation Required
Bus Local and inter-city travel Bus ticket/receipt
Train Domestic travel Train ticket/receipt
Flight Domestic travel Flight ticket/receipt
Taxi/Cab Local conveyance, airport transfers Taxi receipt/bill
Auto Rickshaw Local conveyance Auto receipt (if available)
Personal Vehicle Local conveyance Fuel receipt or mileage log
Metro Local conveyance in metro cities Metro card/ticket
Two Wheeler Local conveyance Fuel receipt or mileage log
Car Pooling Local and domestic travel Payment receipt or app screenshot

How to Submit Travel Expenses

Step-by-Step Process
  1. Select an expense head that includes travel (e.g., Local Conveyance, Domestic Travel)
  2. Fill in the basic expense details (amount, description)
  3. Select the appropriate Travel Type (Local or Domestic)
  4. Choose the Travel Mode from the dropdown
  5. Enter the From Location (starting point)
  6. Enter the To Location (destination)
  7. Select the Travel Date (must be within expense period)
  8. For Local/Domestic travel, enter the Distance in KM
  9. Upload supporting documents (tickets, receipts, etc.)
  10. Review and submit the expense
Important Notes
Travel Type Selection
  • Local: Same city travel
  • Domestic: Different cities in India (Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, etc.)
Date Requirements
  • Travel date must be within the expense period
  • Cannot be a future date
  • Cannot be more than 30 days in the past
Documentation
  • Always upload travel tickets/receipts
  • For personal vehicle, upload fuel receipts
  • Keep mileage logs for personal vehicle use

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Validation Errors
  • Missing Travel Fields: Forgetting to fill travel mode, from/to locations, or travel date
  • Invalid Date: Selecting a future date or date outside the expense period
  • Missing Distance: Not entering distance for Local/Domestic travel
  • Wrong Travel Type: Selecting Domestic for same-city travel
Best Practices
  • Be Specific: Use exact locations (e.g., "Akanksha School, Mumbai" not just "Mumbai")
  • Upload Documents: Always attach tickets, receipts, or fuel bills
  • Accurate Distance: Use Google Maps or similar to get exact distances
  • Clear Description: Explain the purpose of travel in the description field

EPV System User Guide

This comprehensive guide explains how to use the EPV system for different user roles.

Regular User Guide

Submitting an Expense

  1. Log in to the EPV system using your Google account
  2. From the dashboard, click "Create New EPV"
  3. Fill in the expense details (date range, cost center, etc.)
  4. Add individual expense items with descriptions and amounts
  5. Upload supporting documentation (receipts, invoices, etc.)
  6. Review all information and click "Submit"
  7. Your expense will be sent to the appropriate approver

Submitting a Split Invoice

  1. Select Split Invoice as the invoice type in the expense form.
  2. Enter the master invoice details and upload the main invoice document.
  3. Add allocations for each cost center, specifying the amount and approver for each.
  4. Ensure the total allocated amount matches the invoice total (system will validate).
  5. Submit the split invoice. Each allocation will be sent to its respective approver.
  6. Track the status of each sub-invoice in the EPV Records page.

Tracking Your Expenses

  1. From the dashboard, click "EPV Records"
  2. View the status of all your submitted expenses, including split and standard invoices
  3. Click on any EPV ID to view detailed information, including sub-invoice status for splits
  4. Status badges indicate where your expense is in the workflow

Handling Rejected Expenses & Supplementary Documents

  1. If your expense is rejected, you'll receive an email notification
  2. Log in to the EPV system and view the rejected expense
  3. Read the rejection reason provided by the approver or finance team
  4. If Finance requests additional documents:
    • Click "Upload Missing Documents" button
    • Upload the requested documents and add a description for each
    • The system will merge your new documents with the original PDF and resubmit to Finance
  5. If rejected with "Restart the whole process" option:
    • Create a new EPV with corrected information

Split Invoice Guide

What is a Split Invoice?

A split invoice allows you to allocate a single invoice across multiple cost centers, each with its own approver and allocation amount. This is useful for shared expenses.

How to Submit a Split Invoice

  1. Choose Split Invoice in the expense form.
  2. Enter the master invoice details and upload the invoice file.
  3. Add allocations for each cost center, specifying the amount and approver.
  4. Ensure the total allocated matches the invoice total (system will validate).
  5. Submit the split invoice. Each allocation is routed to its approver.

Tracking Split Invoices

  • View the master invoice and all sub-invoices in EPV Records.
  • Each sub-invoice shows its cost center, amount, and approval status.
  • Notifications are sent for each approval or rejection.

Supplementary Documents Guide

When are Supplementary Documents Needed?

  • Finance may request additional documents if the original submission is incomplete or unclear.
  • You will receive an email and see a prompt in the EPV record.

How to Upload Supplementary Documents

  1. Click the "Upload Missing Documents" button in the EPV record.
  2. Upload the requested files and add a description for each.
  3. Click "Submit". The system merges your new documents with the original PDF and resubmits to Finance.
  4. Finance is notified and will review the updated submission.

Tracking Supplementary Documents

  • All supplementary documents are listed in the EPV record for reference.

Partial Payment Guide (Finance)

What is a Partial Payment?

Partial payment allows Finance to split a payment into two parts, each with its own details and FCRA status. This is useful for complex or staged payments.

How to Process a Partial Payment

  1. In the Finance Entry form, check the "Partial Payment" box.
  2. Enter details for both payments: journal entry, payment voucher, amount, and FCRA status.
  3. Ensure the sum of both amounts matches the EPV total (system will validate).
  4. Submit the entry. Both payments will be tracked and must be completed for full processing.

Tracking Partial Payments

  • Partial payment details are visible in the EPV record and finance approval screens.

Settings Management

Who Can Update Settings?

  • Only Super Admins and Finance Approvers can update finance settings.

How to Update Settings

  1. Go to the Settings page from the main menu.
  2. Update values such as maximum days past, max reminder days, and academic year.
  3. All changes are logged in the Settings Change Log for audit purposes.

Dashboard & Validation Tips

Using the Dashboard

  • Finance dashboard has tabs for pending, resubmitted, rejected, and processed expenses.
  • Use filters for city, cost center, and status to find records quickly.
  • Click "Reset Filters" to clear all filters and show all records.

Validation & Error Handling

  • All forms have built-in validation. Follow on-screen messages to correct errors.
  • For split invoices, allocation amounts must match the invoice total.
  • For partial payments, both payment amounts must add up to the EPV total.
  • File uploads must be valid and within size/type limits.

The standard processing time for expenses is 5 business days after manager approval. However, several factors can affect processing time:

  • Weekends and holidays: Processing days exclude Saturdays and Sundays.
  • Complex expenses: Expenses with multiple items or special approval requirements may take longer to process.

  1. Environment Setup: Install Python 3.8+ and required packages
